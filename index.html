How status is calculated

Status combines two signals and shows the worse of the two: Multiplier Variance (actual PTD multiplier minus goal — red below -0.30, yellow below 0, green at or above goal) and WIP (red above $1,000 unbilled, green below -$1,000, yellow in between). A job sitting right on its goal multiplier but carrying a large positive WIP will show red, since unbilled revenue is a real risk even when the multiplier looks fine.

Pending (dashed gray) means revenue booked so far is under 2% of the project's total Fee — not enough billed activity yet to judge fairly. This covers the case where a second survey round hasn't been invoiced, hours haven't landed, or a subcontractor hasn't billed you yet. These are held out of the Green/Watch/Under Goal counts rather than defaulted to a color that might not mean anything yet.

Labor / Expense / Accessible Remaining come from the individual task lines under each project. Lines starting with "Sub" are subcontractor budgets — fixed price, not reallocatable, excluded from "accessible." Lines with "Expense" in the name go to the Expense bucket. Everything else defaults to Labor. Remaining = that bucket's budgeted Fee minus cost booked to date. Click any row to see the exact breakdown.

A/R Balance is shown as its own sortable column rather than folded into status, since what counts as concerning A/R depends on your own collections norms.

Trend history is stored only in this browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere). Use Export/Import history to move it to another computer or keep a backup.
