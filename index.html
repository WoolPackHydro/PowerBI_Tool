How status and budget-remaining are calculated

Status combines two signals and shows the worse of the two: Multiplier Variance (actual PTD multiplier minus goal — red below -0.30, yellow below 0, green at or above goal) and WIP (red if positive/unbilled, green if negative, yellow at exactly zero). A job sitting right on its goal multiplier but carrying a large positive WIP will show red, since unbilled revenue is a real risk even when the multiplier looks fine.

Labor / Expense / Accessible Remaining come from the individual task lines under each project (not shown in the summary export you're used to). Each task line is classified by name: lines starting with "Sub" are subcontractor budgets — fixed price, not reallocatable, excluded from "accessible." Lines with "Expense" in the name go to the Expense bucket. Everything else (Labor, Project Management, processing/QC/reporting task lines, markups) defaults to the Labor bucket. Remaining = that bucket's total budgeted Fee minus its cost booked to date. Click any row to see the exact task-line breakdown and check the classification yourself.

A/R Balance is shown as its own sortable column rather than folded into status, since a comfortable receivables balance depends on your own collections norms — there's no one-size threshold built in.

Trend history is stored only in this browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere). Use Export/Import history to move it to another computer or keep a backup.
